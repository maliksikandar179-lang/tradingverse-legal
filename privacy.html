Privacy Policy

TradingVerse Discord server and TradingVerse Bot · Effective 19 October 2026

This policy explains what the TradingVerse Bot ("the bot") stores about you, why, and what you can do about it. We keep only what is needed to run the server and its paid access.

1. What we store

  • Discord identity: your Discord user ID, username and display name.
  • Membership: your plan, start and end dates, and member events (joined, left, renewed, expired, removed).
  • Payments: plan, amount, currency, payment method, payment date and the provider's payment reference. We do not see or store card numbers — card payments are processed by our checkout provider (Whop), which may share your Discord username and email with us so the payment can be matched to you.
  • Courses: which course you bought and when it was delivered.
  • Messages: the bot does not keep a log of member chat. In the private hedge-support channel, the text and screenshots you send are read so the bot can reply. When someone other than the owner posts in a locked channel, the post is removed and a short note of it is sent to the admin.

2. Why we use it

  • To give you the roles and channels your plan includes, and remove them when it ends.
  • To match a payment to the right member and keep a record of it.
  • To deliver courses and keep the server secure.

3. Who else processes it

  • Discord — the platform everything runs on.
  • Whop — card checkout.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — messages and screenshots sent in the hedge-support channel are passed to Anthropic's AI service to produce the bot's reply.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

Membership and payment records are kept while you are a member and afterwards as a payment history, so a renewal or a dispute can be checked. If your Discord account is deleted, your subscriber record is removed automatically.

5. Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by messaging the admin on Discord. Payment records may be kept where we need them for our own accounting. Leaving the server stops any new data being collected.

6. Security

Data is stored on a private machine run by the TradingVerse admin, and only the admin can access it.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
